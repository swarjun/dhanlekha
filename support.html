Frequently asked questions

How do I split an expense with friends?

Open the Shared section and create a friend ledger or a group/trip split. Add an expense, choose who paid and how it's split, and DhanLekha keeps a running balance of who owes whom.

How does “settle up” work?

When balances are outstanding, tap Settle up to record a payment between members. The balance updates for everyone in the ledger, and a fully-paid ledger is marked settled.

What is an Event Fund (kitty)?

For events like birthdays or trips, switch a group into Event Fund mode. Members pay in advances, expenses are spent from the shared pool, and everyone sees Collected, Spent, and Remaining live. Anyone who paid in more than their share can be refunded during settle-up.

How do I share a ledger with a friend?

Add the friend to a ledger using their phone number. If they also use DhanLekha, the shared ledger and its transactions sync to their device so both of you see the same balances. Pull to refresh in the Shared tab to sync the latest entries.

Where is my data stored? Is it private?

Your personal (non-shared) transactions are stored only on your device. Shared ledgers sync securely through Firebase so participants can settle up. Read the full details in our Privacy Policy.

How do I export my transactions or reports?

Open the Reports tab, choose a report and date range, and export it to PDF or Excel to share or keep for your records.

How do I set a budget or a reminder?

Set monthly category limits from Budgets, and create one-time or recurring bill/payment reminders from the bell icon. Reminders notify you before something is due.

Which languages does DhanLekha support?

English, हिंदी (Hindi), and मराठी (Marathi). Change the language anytime in Settings.

Why do I see ads, and how do I remove them?

Ads help keep DhanLekha free. You can remove them by upgrading to a premium plan within the app.

How do I delete my account and data?

Use the in-app Delete Account option, or follow the steps on our Account Deletion page.
